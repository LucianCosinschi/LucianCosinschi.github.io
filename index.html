I design the systems that produce defensible evidence that a person can do a task — and the governance that keeps that evidence standing when someone audits it. I think of this as architecture rather than assessment: I build the calibration and verification layer, and let domain experts judge the domain.

This site is where I keep my own working documents in the open — thoughts, open questions, and research notes — alongside a short shelf of things worth reading. Most of it is unfinished on purpose.
